Welcome to Ledgery

Track what you buy, what you sell, and what you actually make. Everything is saved in this browser.

  1. Add something you bought. Put in what you paid, so profit can be worked out when it sells.
  2. Record a sale. Fees are estimated for you. You can also add a new item right in the sale form.
  3. See what you make. Your Dashboard and Analytics update as you go: profit, margins, best platforms and how fast things sell.

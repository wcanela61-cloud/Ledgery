Privacy Policy

Last updated September 28, 2026

The short version

  • Your inventory, sales and photos belong to you. Only you can see them.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics tools.
  • You can export everything or delete your account at any time from Settings.

This policy explains what Ledgery ("we", "us") collects when you use the Ledgery app, why, and the choices you have. By using Ledgery you agree to this policy.

What we collect

Account details

  • Email address and password. Used to sign you in and to send account emails (confirming your email, resetting your password). Passwords are stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider; we can never see them.
  • Your name and shop name, if you enter them, to personalize the app.

Your ledger

  • Everything you enter: inventory items, item codes, costs, sales, fees, expenses, goals, and your settings.
  • Photos you attach to items. They are shrunk on your device before they are saved.

What we don't collect

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and we don't collect your location, contacts, or payment card details.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show your dashboard, calculate profit, and sync your ledger between the devices you sign in on.
  • To send account emails you ask for (confirmation and password reset).
  • To keep the service secure and to fix problems.

We don't sell, rent or share your data for marketing, and we don't use it to train AI models.

Where your data is stored

  • On your device. Ledgery keeps a copy of your ledger and photos in your browser's storage so it opens quickly and works offline.
  • In your account. When you're signed in, your ledger and photos are stored with Supabase, our database and sign-in provider. Access rules on the database only let your own signed-in account read or change your rows.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase: accounts, sign-in emails and data storage.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website files. Like any web host, it may log basic request information such as IP addresses.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr: deliver fonts and the sign-in library. Your browser contacts them directly, so they receive your IP address and browser details.

Ledger, the built-in assistant

On the Ledgery website, Ledger's answers are worked out on your own device from your ledger; your data is not sent to an AI service. If this ever changes, we'll update this policy and tell you in the app first.

Cookies and local storage

Ledgery uses your browser's local storage to remember your ledger, your settings and whether you're signed in. These are needed for the app to work; there are no advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: Settings → Data & backup lets you download your whole ledger, and CSV exports of inventory and sales.
  • Correct: you can edit anything you've entered at any time.
  • Delete: Settings → Account → Delete account permanently removes your login, your ledger and your photos from our servers. You can also clear data on a device by signing out and clearing your browser's site data.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have additional rights, such as to object to processing or to complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us and we'll help.

How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your ledger and photos are removed from our database right away; copies in our providers' routine backups expire on their own schedule. Photos you remove from items are cleaned up automatically.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protected by per-account access rules. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password that you don't use anywhere else.

Children

Ledgery is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make meaningful changes, we'll update the date above and let you know in the app before they take effect.

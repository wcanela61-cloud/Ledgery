Welcome to Ledgery

Track what you buy, what you sell, and what you actually make. Everything is saved in this browser.

  1. Add something you bought. Put in what you paid, so profit can be worked out when it sells.
  2. Record a sale. Fees are estimated for you. You can also add a new item right in the sale form.
  3. See what you make. Your Dashboard and Analytics update as you go: profit, margins, best platforms and how fast things sell.

Platform fees

Used to pre-fill fees when you record a sale
Starting values are US rates as of September 2026. Platforms change fees often, so check yours and edit these. Poshmark charges a flat $2.95 on sales under $15. You can always overwrite the fee on any sale.

Your data lives only in this browser. Clearing browser data, using a private window or switching devices will lose it, so download a backup now and then.
